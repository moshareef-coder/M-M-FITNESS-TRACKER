Unio · the talking figure

Everything he says

Every line the figure on the session screen can say, grouped by the moment that triggers it. This reads straight from quips.mjs, the same file the live app imports, so nothing here can drift out of step with what actually ships.

What he will not do. Joke about your body, the scale, your numbers, a set you missed or a day you skipped, or you against your partner. He can be warm and specific about your goal and your streak, which is different from being clever at your expense — when in doubt, he is the idiot in the scene, never you. He never gives form or medical advice.

Nothing matches that.
