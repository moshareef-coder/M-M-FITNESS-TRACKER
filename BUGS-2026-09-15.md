# Unio bug audit, 2026-09-15

Six parallel audits, run read-only while three other sessions were editing. Nothing here
was fixed: the owner asked for the list first so he can choose what gets touched.

Context: 122 commits since 2026-09-12, index.html 15,200 to 23,246 lines, the app renamed
Fit Together to Unio, and native iOS with widgets, Live Activity, Sign in with Apple and
APNs all landed. Days from submission.

Full reports (with reproductions, queries and 301 screenshots) live in the session
scratchpad under `audit-3/`: ENGINE.md, FEATURES.md, NATIVE.md, RENAME.md, SECURITY.md,
UI.md, and `uidrive/shots/`.

Every item below was reproduced or proven against live code or the live database. Nothing
here is a suspicion.

---

## A. Ship-stoppers: security, and a published policy that is currently false

**A1. The partnership escalation is still open.** `20260911_rls_hardening.sql` was never run:
zero of its four triggers and zero of its six policies exist. `partnerships` INSERT is still
`with check is_me(inviter_email)` with no constraint on `status`. One POST with the public
anon key names any victim as an accepted partner and grants read on their profile,
weigh-ins, logs, workouts and photos.

**A2. The group model is the same attack without the cap, and it is new.** `can_see()` grew a
group branch that checks `left_at` and never `accepted_at`. Create a group, add yourself, add
any list of victims with `accepted_at` preset: four requests, unbounded victims, where the
partnership path is capped at one by a `limit 1`. `assign_workout` then lets you overwrite a
victim's plan for any date. **The existing migration does not cover this.** Proven read-only
against production.

**A3. Progress photos are readable by a partner at the storage layer.** Proven live, signed in
as the real partner account: 9 of 9 `body/` objects and 9 of 9 `body_photos` rows readable.
`privacy.html` says "Progress photos are visible only to you" and "restricted at the server".
Both statements are currently false in a published document.

**A4. `delete-account` was never deployed.** Still v7 from 2026-08-30, the pre-hardening build.
It leaves 11 tables behind including `push_subscriptions` and `apns_tokens`, so a deleted
account keeps receiving push forever. That is the substance of Guideline 5.1.1(v).

**A5. `redeem_invite_code` creates an accepted partnership with no consent step**, and nothing
rate-limits PostgREST. 887M code space is hours of brute force at real scale. It also survives
the A1 trigger fix, because SECURITY DEFINER bypasses triggers.

**A6. `CRON_SECRET` is a literal inside the body of `notify_live_session_start()`**, and it is
the only gate on two destructive functions deployed with `verify_jwt = false`. Rotate it.

## B. Features that are live and do not work

**B1. The goal weight and duration are collected and thrown away.** The onboarding target step
builds goal weight, height, duration and pace; the Start button calls `finishOnboarding` with a
fresh object literal containing none of them (`index.html:8285`). Proven end to end: 200 to 170
at "Gentle, 7 months" produces a plan screen reading "for 12 weeks, Pace Steady", a `user_goals`
row with no metric, start or target, no height on the profile, and no weigh-in. `weightGoalFor()`
therefore still returns null for everyone and the goal line, Goal stat and projection still never
render, which is exactly what that commit claimed to fix. Two bugs nested inside: `lossTimelines`
emits `gentle` while `PACE` expects `easy`, so Gentle silently stores "steady"; and the plan
screen prints a stale `p.weeks` rather than the duration just chosen.

**B2. Two of the eight goals do nothing.** `adapter.mjs`'s `TREE_CHILDREN` still lists only the
nine old bubbles, so `build-endurance` and `move-better` fail `isValidBubble`, fall back to the
legacy string and both resolve to Stay consistent. Build endurance loses its 3x35 min cardio for
1x20 easy; Move better gets no mobility block, which is the whole point of it. Live in production,
vendored copy included. Visible in ten seconds: the confirm screen says "Built for just be
consistent."

**B3. The Focus areas settings page does nothing.** `saveBodyFocus` writes `[["chest",3],["quads",2]]`;
every reader expects `"chest:3"` strings, confirmed by calling the engine's `parseFocus` directly
(returns empty). Picks vanish on reopen, the row reads "Not set", the generator ignores them, and
the success toast names seven muscles while two are written. The column is `text[]`, so production
may reject the write outright.

**B4. Any workout crossing midnight reports zero.** Sets are written with `sessionDate()` and the
wrap-up reads them with `todayStr()`. Observed: "Workout Ended, 0 of 5 exercises, 0/5, under 1m"
directly above a toast reading "NEW PR 185 lb, Bench Press". `doMarkGymDayDone` gets this right and
says so in a comment; `todaysPRLogs`, `planVolumeLb` and `todaysLoggedExerciseNames` were missed.

**B5. "Generate for me" ignores the suggestion 40px above it.** The card says "Chest and Shoulders,
99% match", the button builds "Leg day". `chooseGenerateBtn` calls `generateWorkout()` with no
arguments. Reproduced on four scenarios.

**B6. APNs is hardcoded to the sandbox.** `index.html:22090` writes `environment: "development"`
and the sender routes on that column to Apple's sandbox gateway. A distribution build registers a
production token, so Apple returns `BadDeviceToken`. Cable installs keep working, which is exactly
how this reaches launch day undetected.

## C. Native surfaces that leak or corrupt data

**C1. Lock Screen set logs are a global queue with no owner and no expiry.** `LogSetIntent.swift`
appends bare timestamps. The drain returns early when no session is live, runs only on
`visibilitychange` (never on cold launch), and is keyed to neither account nor date. Nothing ever
clears it: sign-out only touches `ft_`-prefixed localStorage and the bridge has no clear method.
So yesterday's taps land as phantom sets in today's workout, and one person's taps get written
under the next person to sign in on that phone. Same shape as the offline-queue bug already fixed
once, on a surface that fix never reached.

**C2. The widget keeps drawing the previous user after sign-out and the deleted user after
deletion.** Name, partner name, both weekly counts, streak. Nothing overwrites the snapshot in
either path. Privacy, and 5.1.1(v) exposure.

**C3. The Live Activity can be stranded on the Lock Screen four ways.** It is held in an instance
variable and `end()` gives up when that is nil; nothing ever reads `Activity.activities`. Force
quit strands it and a resumed session requests a second one; an abandoned workout never reaches
the only path that ends it; `discardSession()` calls `endLive()` (the presence row) rather than
`endLiveActivity()`, a different function with a similar name, and then nulls the session so
nothing can ever end it; sign-out and deletion just reload.

**C4. Sign in with Apple's private relay creates a second account and strands the first.**
Everything keys on email: `MY_EMAIL`, `profiles.eq("email")`, group membership, storage paths
`{email}/...`. A relay address is a different string. Pair via Google, later tap Apple (which is
first, per Guideline 4.8), and you land in an empty account while your partner's app still points
at the old one. An invite sent to the real address cannot be accepted from relay. Deleting one
leaves the other. There is no code-only fix; the real answer is keying on `auth.users.id`.

**C5. Hardware back exits the app mid-workout.** No `pushState`, no `popstate` listener, no
Capacitor `backButton` handler anywhere. `history.back()` during a session lands on sign-in with
the session gone.

## D. Regressions from the last three days

**D1. The honest clock cut default weekly volume about 15 percent.** Sweep baseline against HEAD:
intermediate groups under 0.8 of target 18.2% to 39.0%, advanced 15.1% to 36.8%, over-budget
warnings 1,319 to 3,226. On a realistic matrix, 17.20 to 14.69 sets a day, six times the 0.4/day
the commit claimed. The trim ladder got stricter and the fill pass that would give sets back is
gated on `askedMinutes !== null`, so it never runs for anyone who has not set a session length,
which is nearly everyone.

**D2. Dropping sub-goals changed the Progress headline for seven child ids.** The commit said they
"resolved to the same kind their bubble gives anyway"; verified false by calling `headlineKindFor`
live. `lose-and-build` recomp to scale (the file's own comment says leading a recomp with the scale
is wrong on purpose), `build-overall` scale to volume, `build-skinny-fat` recomp to volume,
`first-pullup` and `first-pushup` reps to habit, `run-5k` and `faster-mile` endurance to habit.
The entire "reps" headline is now unreachable dead code.

**D3. An existing user's Settings Goals page shows no goal and the first save destroys their
extras.** `GOAL_TILES` dropped `do-a-thing`, `event` and `get-back`. Such a profile opens the page
with nothing selected and a disabled button reading "Pick a goal first";
`normaliseGoalSecondary` coerces every secondary child to null and drops any secondary on a retired
bubble, then writes that lossy list back without warning.

**D4. The update check has been dead since the rename.** `index.html:22785` matches
`/fit-together-([0-9.]+)/` against a worker that now says `unio-`, so `ensureLatestBuild()` returns
empty every time and the "a newer build is live" path never fires. `bump-version.mjs` and
`boot-check.mjs` were both updated, which is why the drift check still passes and nothing flagged
it. Web and PWA only; moot inside the wrap.

**D5. The ten minute mobility block is unreachable**, and Move better is the goal that cannot get
it. The block gates on the child id being `flexibility` or `mobility`; the new picker always writes
`goal_child: null`. The picker's own copy promises an extra goal can add it.

**D6. A stale `goal_detail` keeps steering the engine and the picker can never clear it.**
`renderGoalPage` writes it only `if (child)`, which is now never, while `mapGoal` parses it
unconditionally. A user with "lose 30 lbs by June" who switches to Build muscle is told "30 lb at a
healthy pace is about 52 weeks", as a gain.

## E. Smaller, still real

- **Three different streak numbers at once** in one scenario: Home 1, Progress 2, Profile 4. Three
  functions; `homeStreak()`'s own comment says this must not happen.
- **The ramp line shows the working weight.** "warm-up set, 165 lb x 2" while the dial reads 185,
  and two taps on plus silently move the working target to 190 with the ramp line still on screen.
- **The engine reserves about three minutes a session for ramp rungs the app renders only one of.**
- **Double-tapping onboarding's Start writes two active `user_goals` rows.**
- **Setup Goal reads "Not set" until you open and close any subpage.**
- **The pace step asks "12 / 8 / 16 weeks" of every non-weight goal.**
- **Cardio is prescribed and printed and nothing else.** A build-endurance user is told "3x 35 min
  this week" on every session while Progress says they have not logged a run in four weeks.
- **`apns_tokens` has `UNIQUE(token)` with upsert on that column.** On a shared or resold device
  user B cannot register and user A's nudges keep arriving on B's phone. Zero rows today.
- **Password reset is broken in the native app only.** The deep link handler never reads
  `type=recovery`, so `setSession` emits `SIGNED_IN` and the set-password screen never renders. It
  works on the website, which is why it will not get caught.
- **Apple's name is discarded on first authorization.** `scopes: "email name"` is requested and only
  `identityToken` is read. Apple never sends the name again.
- **The share card still says FIT TOGETHER** (`index.html:10617`), in accent green beside the old
  vercel URL, on the app's only acquisition surface. The same function already says Unio in the
  share text.
- **The launch screen is stock Capacitor art**, a white field with a small blue dumbbell, flashing
  before a near-black app on every cold launch.
- **A third product name, "Creativelab1 Fitness", sits in `project.pbxproj`.** Inert while
  `GENERATE_INFOPLIST_FILE = NO`, but it becomes the home screen name the moment anyone lets Xcode
  migrate to a generated Info.plist.
- **Body tab muscle rows are 18.8px tall and tappable**, against a 44px house minimum.
- **`demo.mjs --check` now exits 1** on two tree coverage gaps. It was 0.
- **A standalone session generator** (cf98627) is committed and unwired.

## What came back clean, so it is not re-audited

- 285 engine tests, sweep and fuzz all pass, and the vendored copy that actually ships matches
  source exactly.
- **Calibration still runs.** Measured: beating the plan twice moves a squat 275 to 285; missing
  reps gives back-off to 265 and drops the day 18 sets to 14; three swaps remove a movement.
- Zero horizontal overflow and zero console errors across 13 scenarios in both themes.
- The partner privacy gate held on all six render paths driven. That recurring class looks closed.
- Swipe to dismiss works on all seven sheets. Log-set and weigh-in are double-tap safe.
- App Group identifiers match in all six places. No secrets committed. Both storage buckets private.
  RLS enabled on all 25 tables. No XSS found.
- Every icon exists at its claimed size with no alpha. Privacy and support pages ship in the binary
  and resolve live.
- No irreversible identifier problem: bundle id, URL scheme, APNs topic and Apple client id were all
  correctly left alone, and no existing user loses local data to the rename.

---

## The two blind spots worth fixing in the instruments, not the code

1. **The engine gate enumerates goals from `goal-tree.json`'s nine bubbles**, so sweep, fuzz and the
   test suite never exercise the two new goals. That is why B2 stayed green. Same shape as the
   blind spot that hid dead calibration for months: the instrument only looks where it already knew
   to look.
2. **Nothing tests the client and the engine against each other.** B2, B3 and D2 are all one bug
   class: the two halves disagreeing about a stored value's shape or vocabulary, with each half
   passing its own tests. A contract test that round-trips what the client writes through what the
   engine parses would have caught all three.

# Resolution, 2026-09-16

Everything above was fixed the following day by eight parallel agents, except the
items listed under "Still waiting" and "Left for a decision". Gate at the end:
293 engine tests pass, `demo --check` exits 0, sweep exits 0 with no FAILs and
nothing KNOWN OPEN, fuzz exits 0 clean, the vendored copy that ships matches
source, and `boot-check` passes.

Several findings turned out to be worse than the audit described, and those are
worth reading even if the rest is not.

## Worse than reported

- **B4, midnight.** The "0 of 5 exercises" was the second path. The FIRST finish
  crashed: `loadAll()` set `TODAY_WORKOUT = null` underneath a live session and the
  wrap-up died on `.exercises`, with every set already saved. Eight readers were on
  the wrong date, not three. Four others were deliberately left, including Home's
  "did I train today", which genuinely means today.
- **B1, the goal weight.** `lossTimelines` emitted `gentle` where the column's own
  check constraint only accepts `easy`, so the real database would have rejected
  the row outright. It only looked half-working in the sandbox.
- **B3, focus areas.** Three bugs, not one: the write shape, whole body writing an
  empty array, and the picker seeding from its own in-memory state (empty on every
  fresh launch) then destructuring stored strings one character at a time.
- **B5, the generate button.** Passing the suggestion through would have been a
  silent no-op: the card and the engine disagree about what a focus IS. The engine
  parses five day patterns and "Chest and Back" matches none.
- **D3, the lost extras.** The cause was `normaliseGoalSecondary` validating against
  `tile.kids`, which is empty on every tile since sub-goals were dropped, so
  everything failed validation and was wiped.
- **A6, the cron secret.** The same literal is also in three `cron.job` command
  strings, not just the function body.
- **The share card.** The dead brand name was the smaller half: the card is set in
  `Sora`, which `index.html` stopped loading, so every workout ever shared went out
  in Helvetica.
- **My own 09-11 migration would have enforced nothing.** Every trigger in it was
  declared SECURITY DEFINER and gated on `current_user not in ('authenticated',
  'anon')`. Inside a SECURITY DEFINER function `current_user` is always the owner,
  so the gate was true on every call and each trigger returned without checking.
  It would have installed clean, verified as present, and done nothing. It was
  never run, which is the only reason that is not worse.

## Found while fixing, not in the audit

- `respond_to_pair_invite` marked the OTHER pending requests `declined` when you
  accepted one. Under the new consent block that would have permanently barred
  anyone who asked you on the day you paired with somebody else, off a refusal you
  never made.
- `leave_my_group()` and `remove_group_member()` also wrote `declined`, so walking
  out of a pair group would have barred those two from ever pairing again.
- `redeem_invite_code` never looked at declined rows, so a refused person could
  re-ask forever. The rate limiter counts failures and a valid code lookup is not
  one.
- `notify-live-start` carries a byte-identical copy of the APNs sender with the same
  environment bug, so fixing only `send-nudges` would have left partner-live pushes
  dead.
- `send-nudges` and `notify-live-start` are deployed with `verify_jwt = true` while
  cron sends a bearer secret that is not a JWT, so those pushes are almost certainly
  dead in production right now. Someone lost `--no-verify-jwt`.
- `personalDayStreak` counted only gym days, so a logged rest day broke a streak the
  app's own info card promises rest days hold.
- `scripts/make-states.mjs` throws on a renamed function, so that page cannot be
  regenerated.

## Deliberately not done, and why

- **The share URL still says `m-m-fitness-tracker.vercel.app`.** No Unio domain
  exists. A dead link on the only acquisition surface is worse than an old name.
- **The widget bundle id stays `...fittogether.FitTogetherWidget`.** Renaming target
  names and entitlements files days before submission buys an identifier no user
  reads, whose parent is permanently `fittogether` anyway. The display name was
  fixed instead.
- **Ungating the volume fill pass.** Built and measured: it recovers real volume but
  made asking for arm focus REDUCE biceps from 10 weekly sets to 8, and bought 0.3
  points on the headline metric. Two sweep FAILs is not that trade.
- **The second main's ramp rung, unconditionally.** Cost 319 over-budget days and
  164 working sets for the one claim in that research section with no citation.
- **Two em dashes in `test.mjs`** live inside the regex that ASSERTS no exercise cue
  contains a dash. Removing them removes the check.

## Left for a decision

- **Re-derive `sessionMin`.** Honest costing moves three goals from the 45 minute
  chip to the 60 minute one, Lose weight included.
- **The Apple relay mitigation.** `result.response.user` is a stable per-app Apple id
  returned on every authorization. Storing it and consulting it before keying on
  email stops the duplicate account without re-keying anything. It does not merge
  two accounts that already exist.
