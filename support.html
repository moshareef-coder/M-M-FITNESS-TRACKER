Common questions

How do I pair with my partner?

Open Setup and share your invite code. Your partner enters it on their device and you are linked. Most of the app needs a partner to be useful, so do this first.

Why has my rank not gone up?

Ranks are shared. Neither of you advances until you both qualify, which is the whole point: you are as strong as your weakest link. Your rank card will tell you when you are waiting on your partner, and what they still need.

I logged a workout at the gym and it did not appear.

If your phone lost signal, the app saves your sets on your device and syncs them when you are back online. You will see a banner telling you how many changes are waiting. Nothing is lost. Reopen the app somewhere with signal and it will catch up.

Do I have to take photos?

No. Workout proof photos and progress photos are both optional and are only ever visible to you and your partner.

How do I delete my account?

Setup, then Delete account. It is immediate and permanent, and it erases everything including photos. See the privacy policy for detail.

What happens if my partner deletes their account?

You are unpaired automatically. Your own data and history stay exactly as they were, and you can pair with someone else.
