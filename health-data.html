Consumer Health Data Policy

Unio · Last updated 4 October 2026

Some of what you put into Unio is health information. This page says exactly which, why we keep it, who it reaches, and how to delete it. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else.

What health information we collect

  • Body weight and the dates you weigh in.
  • Body measurements you choose to log: waist, hips, thigh, arm and chest.
  • Joints that hurt and equipment you do not have, which you mark in Setup, Limits, plus an optional short note.
  • Progress photos, if you take them.
  • Age, sex, height and activity level, if you give them, and your training goals.

We do not collect heart rate, sleep, menstrual or pregnancy data, or anything from Apple Health or other apps.

Why we collect it

Only to plan your training and show your progress: your weight sets starting weights, the joints you mark are left out of your plan, and your measurements and photos are yours to track. We ask for your consent before storing any of it, the first time you save some or before your first workout.

Who it reaches

  • Your partner, only for what you switch on: weigh-ins and measurements under Weigh-ins sharing, and progress photos under photo sharing. Joints that hurt and your note are never shared.
  • Our service providers, who store and serve it on our behalf and may not use it for anything else: Supabase (database and files) and Vercel (hosting).

We never sell health information, and we never share it with advertisers. There is no advertising or analytics software in Unio.

Your rights

  • Delete it. Delete any weigh-in, measurement or photo, clear your limits in Setup, or delete your whole account in Setup, then Delete account. Deleting your account erases all of it, including the files.
  • See it, or withdraw consent. Email us and we will send you a copy, or stop and delete it.
  • Turn sharing off at any time, which stops your partner seeing it from then on.

Contact

Email trainwithunio@gmail.com for any request about your health information. We reply within 45 days, usually much sooner.
