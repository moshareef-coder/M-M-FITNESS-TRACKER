Privacy Policy

Fit Together · Last updated 30 August 2026

Fit Together is a workout tracker built for two people training together. This policy explains exactly what we store, why, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name, from Google Sign-In. We never receive your Google password.
  • Fitness data you enter: body weight, workouts, exercises, sets, reps, and weights lifted.
  • Optional profile details you choose to give: height, age, sex, goals and training preferences. These are used only to tailor your workout suggestions.
  • Photos you choose to upload: workout proof photos and progress photos. Both are optional.

Who can see it

Only you and the one partner you pair with. Your data is isolated at the database level, which means other users of the app cannot read it, not even if they know your email address. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models.

Photos are stored privately. They are served through short-lived links that expire, and are never publicly browsable.

Workout generation

If you use AI-generated workouts, we send your training preferences and basic biometrics (such as age, sex, height, weight and goals) to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the plan. We do not send your name, email, or photos. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider, and is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by per-user security rules enforced by the database itself.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Setup → Delete account inside the app. This immediately and irreversibly erases your profile, workouts, weigh-ins, photos, rank and history, and removes your account entirely. It also unpairs your partner. There is no waiting period and nothing is retained in backups beyond our provider's standard rolling window.

Children

Fit Together is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we will update the date above and note the change in the app.
